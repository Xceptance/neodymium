About VÉRLA APPAREL

Conceived in Verona in 2026, VÉRLA APPAREL redefines luxury legwear and everyday wardrobe essentials. We combine time-honored Italian knitting techniques with innovative, high-recovery microfibers to create garments that deliver effortless shape and durability.

Every piece is designed with sustainability at its core: using regenerated polyamides, certified organic cottons, and non-toxic dye baths.
