How do I choose the correct tights / legwear size?

Refer to our which correlates height (cm/ft) and body weight (kg/lbs) to recommend Size 1 (S), 2 (M), 3 (L), or 4 (XL).

How does the 4+1 Free Multi-Buy promotion work?

Add any 5 qualifying items with the 4+1 Free badge to your shopping bag. The lowest-priced item will automatically be discounted to $0 at checkout.

Can I return items to a physical boutique?

Yes. Online purchases can be returned free of charge within 30 days to any VÉRLA flagship boutique with your order confirmation email.
